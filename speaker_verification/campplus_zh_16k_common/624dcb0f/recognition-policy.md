# Conversation Person recognition trial

This artifact is distinct from the historical admission trial. It returns advisory identity evidence, never admission, authentication or personal-memory permission. Channel reads it alongside the existing shared model; no second model or weights download is needed.

The initial similarity floor 0.55 follows the existing enrollment consistency/trial floor, with an additional 0.10 margin between different Persons. These are conservative trial settings, **not a calibrated false-accept rate**. Multiple templates for the same Person are aggregated before the margin check. The v2 trial uses a 600 ms voiced-speech floor for conversational probes, independently of the 1500 ms enrollment floor. This is a bounded usability trial, not a calibrated short-speech accuracy guarantee. The exact floor is sent to the shared extractor; there is no second hidden conversation duration gate. Neither silence padding nor audio from previous turns counts as new identity evidence. Below 600 ms, report insufficient speech. A compared probe below the similarity threshold remains uncertain/short_speech_inconclusive until it has at least 1500 ms of voiced speech (min_unknown_speech_ms); a weak short probe is not evidence of an unregistered speaker. The similarity floor is not lowered for short probes. All positive decisions remain controlled_trial. Missing assets, model mismatch, ambiguous candidates, dependency failure and expired evidence must never produce a Person.

Before this policy may authorize automatic access to private memory, evaluate held-out A/B/unknown speech recorded on the intended devices, measure false accepts and rejects including short speech, TV/replay, similar voices and overlapping speakers, and publish a separately reviewed policy. A successful deterministic pipeline test is not acoustic validation. The UI must say the voice *may* match; unknown and unavailable remain normal conversation states.
